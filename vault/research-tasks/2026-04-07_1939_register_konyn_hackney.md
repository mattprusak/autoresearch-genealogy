# 1939 Register — Konyn household, Hackney

## Objective

Locate Abraham Konyn (b. 1888-09-11) and Leah Konyn née Berkovitch/Zaidenwaber (b. 1888-09-22 Amsterdam) in the 1939 Register. Both are known to have died in the UK (Leah in Hackney 1960), so they were almost certainly enumerated on 29 September 1939.

## Why this record
