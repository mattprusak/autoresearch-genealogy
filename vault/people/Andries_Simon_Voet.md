**Two Isaac Voets** (b. 1865 and 1870) — common pattern when an older child dies and a later one is named after them. Probably the 1865 Isaac died young and the 1870 Isaac was named after him.

**Two Holocaust victims among the children**: Hartog Voet and Ruben Andries Voet both murdered at **Sobibor on the same date, 26 March 1943** — meaning they were on the **same transport from Westerbork** (Westerbork → Sobibor transports of March 1943 are documented).

Sister Rebecca Voet died Amsterdam 1940-11-21, just six months into the German occupation. Cause unknown — could be natural, could be related.

Two infant deaths (Emanuel, Samuel).
