## Narrative

Born Amsterdam 1888, died Hackney 1960. This places her firmly in the **Amsterdam → London East End** Jewish migration. Hackney was a major destination for Dutch and Eastern European Jews in the late 19th and early 20th centuries; the East End / Hackney Jewish community was well established by the time Leah arrived.

The double maiden name "Berkovitch or Zaidenwaber" is notable:
- **Berkovitch** — Ashkenazi patronymic ("son of Ber"), common in Russian Empire / Poland.
- **Zaidenwaber** (Yiddish: זייִדנוועבער, "silk weaver") — an occupational surname, Eastern European Jewish, specifically associated with textile trades.

Both are Eastern European in character, not Dutch. The likely story: Leah's family migrated from Eastern Europe to Amsterdam (part of the late-19th-century Ashkenazi wave into the Netherlands), where she was born, before the family or Leah herself moved on to London. She married Abraham Konyn (also Dutch-Jewish) and had Henrietta, who eventually married into the Simpson family in England.
