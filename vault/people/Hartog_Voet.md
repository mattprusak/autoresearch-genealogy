## Same transport as his brother

Hartog and his brother **Ruben Andries Voet** were both murdered at Sobibór on **26 March 1943** — they were on the **same transport from Westerbork**. The 23 March 1943 Westerbork → Sobibór transport carried ~1,250 Dutch Jews, almost all of whom were gassed on arrival on 26 March.

**Two confirmed Holocaust victims in Alexander's mother's grandmother's extended family**, plus possibly Rebecca Voet (died Amsterdam Nov 1940 — six months into the German occupation; cause not yet known but the timing is suspicious).

## Relationship to Alexander

Hartog is Alexander's **great-great-great-uncle** (brother of Andries Simon Voet's children, where one of them — possibly Betsy/Betje — would be Alexander's direct ancestor). Verification of the Voet→Pach junction is ongoing.
