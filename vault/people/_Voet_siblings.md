## Holocaust narrative

Hartog (74) and Ruben (68) — two elderly brothers — were deported on the **23 March 1943 Westerbork → Sobibór transport** (one of the standard documented transports; ~1,250 Dutch Jews; almost all gassed on arrival 26 March 1943). They died on the same day in the same place.

Their sister Rebecca died in Amsterdam on **21 November 1940**, six months after the German invasion of the Netherlands. Cause of death unknown — could be natural or could be related to the early occupation. Worth checking Amsterdam death registers and Joods Monument.

If Betsy Voet really is their sister, then Hartog, Ruben, Rebecca, and the other siblings are Alexander's **3x great-uncles/aunts**, and Alexander has **at least two confirmed Holocaust victims in his direct extended family**.
