# Dutch-Jewish line: initial open-source sweep

## Headline findings

1. **Likely Holocaust victim in the family: Ruben Andries Voet (1874 Amsterdam → 1943 Auschwitz)**, plausibly a brother of [[Betsy_Pach]] née Voet, making him a son of [[Andries_Simon_Voet]]. Source: Geni (requires browser verification).
2. **The "Leah Konijn" on Joods Monument page 158655 is NOT Alexander's great-grandmother** — that Leah was daughter of Simon Konijn × Mary Potzer and was murdered in the Holocaust. Our Leah died in Hackney 1960. Consistent with the migration hypothesis.
3. **Neither "Berkovitch" nor "Zaidenwaber" return any hits** in Akevoth, Joods Monument, or openarchieven.nl. This strongly suggests Leah's parents were part of the **1880s Russian/Polish Jewish migration wave** that passed through Amsterdam, and are under-represented in databases focused on long-established Dutch Jewish families. The Amsterdam *bevolkingsregister* (population register) is the most promising next source.
4. **Multiple near-miss Salomon Konijns found, none confirmed:**
   - Salomon Konijn 1874–1932 (Joods Monument 217809) — son of Bram × Grietje Polak. Likely a cousin of our Salomon.
   - Joseph Konijn 1863–1931 (Geni) — same birth year as our Salomon, diamond worker, buried Muiderberg. Possibly a brother.
5. **Pach/Voet branch relationship to the Konyn line remains undetermined.** Hypothesis: they may be an in-law branch rather than direct ancestry, joining the tree via a marriage we haven't mapped yet.

## Concrete next searches (browser, free)

1. **Open each Joods Monument URL above in a browser** and capture: full name, parents, spouse, children, last known address, date and place of deportation, date and place of death. Priority: [Abraham Konijn 435036](https://www.joodsmonument.nl/nl/page/435036/abraham-konijn), [Mietje Konijn 197942](https://www.joodsmonument.nl/nl/page/197942/mietje-konijn), [Salomon Konijn 217809](https://www.joodsmonument.nl/en/page/217809/salomon-konijn), and the Voet pages.
2. **openarchieven.nl / WieWasWie targeted record pulls:**
   - Birth: Abraham Konijn, Amsterdam, 11 Sept 1888 (try ±2 days; try Konyn/Conyn)
   - Death: Mietje van Slochem (or Konijn-van Slochem), Amsterdam, 10 Feb 1911
   - Marriage: Salomon Konijn × Mietje van Slochem, Amsterdam, c.1885–1890
   - Birth: Leah, Amsterdam, c.22 Sept 1888; filter by father surname containing "wab*", "wab*", "berk*", "zeid*"
3. **Stadsarchief Amsterdam bevolkingsregister** — pull the household card for Salomon Konijn × Mietje van Slochem. This will list every child, every address, and "vertrokken naar" (departed to) annotations — critical for confirming London migration and identifying Henrietta's siblings.
4. **ANDB diamond workers' database** ([diamantbewerkers.nl](https://diamantbewerkers.nl/)) — search Konijn, Van Slochem, Voet. Diamond-cutting was the dominant trade and membership is indexed.
5. **FreeBMD**:
   - Leah Konyn death, Hackney, Sep quarter 1960
   - Abraham Konyn death, c.1961
   - Konyn × Berkovitch/Zaidenwaber marriage, London or earlier
6. **Arolsen Archives** ([collections.arolsen-archives.org](https://collections.arolsen-archives.org/)) — search Konijn, Voet, Pach with no forename filter; survivors sometimes appear in DP camp lists.
7. **Yad Vashem Pages of Testimony** — each confirmed victim. Pages of Testimony often list the submitter's name and relationship — this can connect our branch to documented genealogies upstream.
8. **Geni.com merge check** — once our Abraham is anchored to a Geni profile, the entire upstream may already be assembled by other researchers on the big-merge tree.
9. **Pach/Voet junction** — determine *how* Pach/Voet connects. Most likely an in-law branch via a marriage not yet mapped.
