# Web verification pass — Dutch-Jewish leads

## Confirmed

### Voet family structure (Strong-leaning Moderate)

- **Andries Simon Voet** (b. 1838-04-01) married **Leentje Park** (not "Leentje Voet" — Voet is married name). Source: Geni, Dutch Jewry, Genealogie Online.
- Children confirmed: **Ruben Andries Voet** (1874), **Betje/Betsy Voet** (our 1863 Betsy Pach), **Hartog Voet**, **Isaac Voet**, **Rebecca Voet**.
- Leentje's full name possibly "Leentje Isaac Park" (Isaac = her father's given name).

### Holocaust: Ruben Andries Voet

- Born **1874-06-27 Amsterdam**
- Died **1943-03-26 Sobibor** (not Auschwitz as the initial sweep had it)
- Wife: **Naatje Presser**
- Son: **Andries Voet** (b. 1893-06-24 Amsterdam, d. 1942-09-30 Auschwitz) — Joods Monument page 214544
- Sources: Geni profile (https://www.geni.com/people/Ruben-Andries-Voet/6000000019782788100), Dutch Jewry (https://www.dutchjewry.org/genealogy/slagter/952.shtml), Joods Monument (https://www.joodsmonument.nl/en/page/173037/ruben-voet)
- A 1924-12-04 Amsterdam marriage record on openarchieven.nl (https://www.openarchieven.nl/nha:11a0be1f-8266-48bf-83ac-10fd51216d0a/en) references Andries Voet, Ruben Voet, Naatje Presser, Lena Gokkes, Jozef Gokkes, Bloeme Speijer — this is a child of Ruben × Naatje marrying.

### Mozes Konijn × Femmina Kool family (separate from our line, probably)

- Mozes Konijn (1843–1922) × Femmina Samuel Cohen Kool (1843–1886) had these children per genealogical sources:
  - Eva Konijn (1872–1943)
  - Sara Werkheim-Konijn (1874–1943) — married in 1899, openarchieven record
  - Abraham Konijn (1878–1942)
  - Mietje Konijn (1880–1943 Sobibor, Joods Monument page 197942)
  - Rosette Konijn (1883–1943, Joods Monument page 197941)
- **No Salomon b. 1863 listed among their children.** The earlier hypothesis that our Salomon Konyn was a son of this Mozes is **not supported** by available evidence. Our Salomon would need to be from a first wife (none documented) or our tree's "Moses Konyn" is a different individual.
- **However, all five listed children died in 1942–1943, all victims of the Holocaust.** If a connection to our family were ever established, these would be ~four great-great-aunts/uncles murdered.

### Abraham Konijn 1848–1908 (Joods Monument page 435036)

- NOT our Abraham (wrong dates). This Abraham was father of the 1874 Salomon Konijn × Grietje Polak, at Zwanenburgwal 66 in Amsterdam.
- Named "Salomon Abraham Konijn" and "Betje Joseph de Jonge" as his parents.
- This is a separate Konijn branch. Possibly a distant cousin of our Abraham but not on our direct line.
