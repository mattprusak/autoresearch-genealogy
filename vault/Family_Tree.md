## Major outstanding contradictions / data integrity issues

1. **Ethel May Simpson maiden name**: MyHeritage says "Calvert", but her recorded sisters are all "Major" — strongly suggests the canonical name is Major and Calvert is a tree error.
2. **Leah Konyn parents**: maiden name "Berkovitch or Zaidenwaber" but tree places parents as David Pach + Betsy Voet (whose surname is also incompatible).
3. **Mietje Van Slochem parents**: tree places David Pach + Betsy Voet as her parents, but **Betsy was born only 2 years before Mietje** — biologically impossible.
4. **Sophia De Vries vs Salomon Konyn**: Sophia died 1859, Salomon recorded born 1863 — the woman recorded as his mother died before he was born. Salomon is probably the child of Moses's second (unrecorded) wife.
5. **Salomon Konyn birthplace**: CSV says "Hareem, Holland" — almost certainly Haarlem (typo).
6. **Betsy/Betje Voet (b. 1863)**: shown in MyHeritage as a great-great-grandmother and as the daughter of Andries Simon Voet, but she does NOT appear in the CSV's children list for Andries (the 7 listed are Isaac, Hartog, Isaac, Ruben, Rebecca, Emanuel, Samuel).
